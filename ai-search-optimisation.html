What AI search optimisation involves

More people now ask an AI tool who to hire or where to go, and get one answer instead of a list of links. AI search optimisation, also called GEO or AEO, makes sure your business is part of that answer.

Clear facts about your business

Plain, consistent descriptions of who you are, what you do and where, so AI tools do not have to guess.

Answer-focused pages and FAQs

Pages that answer real customer questions directly and clearly, the kind of content AI tools like to quote.

Structured data

Behind-the-scenes markup on your site that tells search engines and AI tools exactly what each page is about.

Consistent mentions online

Your business described the same way on your profiles, listings and other sites, which builds the trust AI tools look for.

Reputation signals

Genuine reviews and mentions that show you are a real, trusted business.

Common Questions

What is GEO or AEO?

GEO (generative engine optimisation) and AEO (answer engine optimisation) both mean making your business easy for AI tools to understand and recommend. It is the AI-era sibling of SEO.

Is it different from SEO?

They overlap a lot. Good SEO helps, but AI tools also look closely at how clearly and consistently your business is described across the web, and whether your pages give direct answers.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. AI answers change and can vary from one question to the next. We make you as clear and trustworthy as possible, which gives you the best realistic chance.

Which AI tools do you work with?

The main ones people use, including ChatGPT, Google's AI answers and Perplexity. The same groundwork helps across all of them.

How do I know whether AI tools mention my business?

We test the questions your customers would ask and show you what the tools say, then repeat the checks so you can see what changes.

Do I need a new website for this?

Often not. We start with what you have and improve it. If your site is genuinely holding you back, we will say so and explain why.
