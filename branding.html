What branding includes

Branding is more than a logo. It is how your business looks, sounds and feels to customers, so they recognise you, remember you and choose you.

Logo design

A clear, memorable logo that suits your business and works on your website, your signage and your social media.

Colours and fonts

A simple set of colours and fonts chosen to fit your business and used consistently everywhere.

Brand voice and messaging

How your business talks: a clear description of what you do, who you help and a tone of voice that sounds like you.

Brand guidelines

A short, easy guide so you, your team and anyone you work with use your brand the same way every time.

Stationery and print

Business cards, letterheads, flyers, menus and signage that all match, supplied as print-ready files.

Online and social branding

Profile images, covers and templates for social media, and a brand your website can be built around.

Common Questions

What is branding, and why does my business need it?

Branding is the overall impression your business makes: its name, logo, colours, wording and how it treats customers. A consistent brand helps customers recognise you, trust you and remember you.

Do I need a new logo, or can you refresh my existing one?

Either. We can refresh what you already have, or start again if it is holding you back. We will tell you honestly which makes more sense.

What will I get at the end?

Your final logo files in the formats you need, your colours and fonts, and short guidelines. We agree exactly what is included, including print items like business cards, before we start.

How long does branding take?

It depends on what is included and how quickly we can agree each step. After the free chat we will give you a realistic timeline.

How many revisions are included?

We agree the number of revisions up front, so you know exactly what to expect before we start.

Can you also build my website?

Yes. Branding and a website work best together, and we can build your site around your new brand.

Do you work with businesses in Rochdale and Manchester?

Yes. We are based in Rochdale and work with businesses across Manchester and Greater Manchester, as well as remotely across the UK.
