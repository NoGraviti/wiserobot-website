What local SEO includes

When someone nearby searches for what you do, Google shows a map and a short list of local businesses first. Local SEO is how you earn a place on that list.

Google Business Profile

Set up, completed and kept accurate, with your services, opening hours, photos and the areas you cover.

Reviews

A simple, steady way to ask happy customers for reviews and to reply to them, which helps your ranking and your reputation.

Local and service-area pages

Genuinely useful pages for each town and service you cover, so you are found where your customers are.

Consistent business listings

Your business name, address and contact details kept the same across the directories and sites Google checks.

Photos, posts and updates

Fresh photos and regular updates on your profile, which show Google and customers that you are active.

A local-ready website

Fast, mobile-friendly pages with the structured information Google uses to understand where you work.

Common Questions

What is local SEO?

Local SEO helps your business show up when people search for what you do in their area, in the Google Maps results and in the standard results. It focuses on your Google Business Profile, your reviews and pages for the places you serve.

Do I need a shop or an office to do local SEO?

No. Many businesses that travel to customers, such as tradespeople and mobile services, appear in local results. We set your profile up as a service-area business and build pages for the places you cover.

How do I get more Google reviews?

By asking at the right moment, making it easy and replying to every review. We help you set up a simple routine. We never buy or fake reviews, which breaks Google's rules.

Can you guarantee a place on the map?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Google decides who appears. We fix what is holding you back and keep improving, which gives you the best realistic chance.

How long does local SEO take?

Some improvements can show within weeks, but steady progress usually builds over several months. After the free check we will tell you honestly what to expect.

We are based in Rochdale and work across Manchester and Greater Manchester, and remotely with businesses anywhere in the UK.
