Common Questions

What is the difference between SMO and SMM?

SMO (social media optimisation) is getting your profiles set up and working well. SMM (social media marketing) is the ongoing work of posting, engaging and running ads. Many businesses want both.

Which platforms should my business be on?

The ones where your customers actually spend time. You do not need to be everywhere, and we will advise you honestly.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay the ad platform directly, so you always control your budget. We plan, manage and report on the campaigns.

How often will you post for me?

We agree a realistic plan up front. Posting consistently matters more than posting constantly.

Can you take over my existing accounts?

Yes, and you always keep ownership of your accounts.

How long before I see results?

Social media builds gradually. We will tell you honestly what to expect for your business after the free chat.
