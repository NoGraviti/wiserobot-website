Common Questions

What is modern SEO, and how is it different from old-school SEO?

Old-school SEO was mostly about ranking in the standard Google results. Modern SEO also covers the Google Maps results, and AI tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI answers that now recommend businesses directly. We work on all of them together.

What is AI search optimisation?

It means making your business easy for AI tools to understand and recommend. That comes down to clear, consistent information about who you are, what you do and where, structured data on your site, and content that answers real questions. It is sometimes called GEO or AEO.

Can you guarantee me first place on Google?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Search engines and AI tools decide the results, not us. What we can do is fix what is holding you back and keep improving it, so you give yourself the best realistic chance.

How long does SEO take to work?

It is not instant. Some fixes can help within weeks, but steady improvement usually builds over several months. We will tell you honestly what to expect for your business after the free SEO check.

Do I need a new website?

Often not. We start with what you have and improve it. If your site is genuinely holding you back, we will say so and explain why before suggesting anything bigger.

Do you only work in Greater Manchester?

No. We are based in Rochdale and happily meet local clients, but SEO can be done entirely remotely, so we work with businesses anywhere in the UK.
