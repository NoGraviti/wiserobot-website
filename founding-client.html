Common Questions

Is the first automation really free?

Yes. For founding clients, the first automation build, connecting up to 3 of your tools, is at no charge. If a software subscription is needed, that cost is yours.

What does 'up to 3 tools' mean?

A tool is any app or service the automation connects, such as your website form, a spreadsheet and your email. We agree exactly which ones, and what the automation will do, in your written plan before we build anything.

Why are you doing this?

WiseRobot is new. We want to do excellent work for a few local businesses, learn from real feedback and show what automation can do.

How do you choose the founding clients?

We look for businesses with a clear, repetitive task we can automate well, and we start with Rochdale and the towns around it. We will reply to every application.

Do I have to buy anything afterwards?

No. If you like the result you can keep it running on a care plan or buy more automations, but there is no obligation.

What kind of automation could I get?

Typical examples are instant replies and follow-up for new enquiries, appointment reminders, payment reminders, and moving information between the tools you already use.

Is my business data safe?

We only ask for access to what the specific automation needs, and you can remove that access at any time.
